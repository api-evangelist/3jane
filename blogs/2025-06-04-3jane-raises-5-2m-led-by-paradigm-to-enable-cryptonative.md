Crypto needs credit expansion. The modern capitalist financial system relies on two core pillars to drive economic growth: a medium of exchange & the creation of credit. While stablecoins have convincingly delivered on the former over the past 10 years, DeFi growth remains constricted by the absence of a scalable and capital-efficient mechanism for credit creation.
