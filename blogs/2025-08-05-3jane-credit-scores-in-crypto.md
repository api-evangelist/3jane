Human will is the most capital-efficient, egalitarian form of collateral ever created. 3Jane is a credit-based money market on Ethereum enabling unsecured lines of credit underwritten against verifiable proofs of DeFi/CEX assets, future cash flows, and credit scores. This unlocks a three-dimensional collateral space within crypto financial markets by introducing future-backed credit alongside existing asset-backed loans.
